CareerPath is a career guidance website that helps students after Class 10 and 12 explore suitable careers and courses based on their interests and skills.
# CareerPath 🚀

> **Discover Your Interests, Build Your Future!**  
> A simple, interactive career guidance website for students after Class 10 and Class 12.

---

## 📌 About The Project

Many students feel confused after writing their 10th and 12th board exams:
- *Which stream should I choose after 10th? (Science, Commerce, or Arts?)*
- *Which college degree and entrance exam should I pick after 12th?*
- *Can I get into tech without hard math?*

**CareerPath** solves this problem! Instead of putting lots of boring text on one long page, it guides students **step-by-step like slides in a presentation**.

---

## 🎬 How It Works (Slide by Slide)

1. **Slide 1: Welcome Page**  
   Introduction to CareerPath with a simple "Get Started" button.

2. **Slide 2: Student Profile**  
   Students select:
   - Current class (**Class 10th** or **Class 12th**)
   - Core interests (*Technology, Science, Business, Arts, Healthcare, Teaching*)
   - Favourite school subjects
   - Key skills and strengths

3. **Slide 3: Career Suggestions**  
   Shows the best matching careers based on the student's profile with match scores (e.g. `⭐ 98% Profile Match`).

4. **Slide 4: Career Details**  
   Click any career to see:
   - What people actually do every day in that job
   - **Salary Meter** (💸💸💸 High / Very High)
   - **Hard Math Meter** (Zero math needed / Basic logic)
   - Step-by-step roadmap from school to first job
   - Required skills, degrees, and entrance exams

5. **Slide 5: Course Recommendations & Next Steps**  
   - **After 10th:** Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce (MEC/CEC), Arts (HEC), and 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma.
   - **After 12th:** Degrees like B.Tech, MBBS, BBA, B.Des, CA, and Law.
   - 3 simple action steps to take this week.

6. **Slide 6: About Us & Ask a Mentor**  
   Project mission, common myths busted, and a contact form to ask questions.

---

## ✨ Main Features

- **One Screen at a Time:** Smooth slide presentation with **Next**, **Back**, and **Home** buttons.
- **Top Progress Bar:** Shows `Step 1 of 5` so students always know where they are.
- **Student-Friendly:** Emojis, bright colors, salary meters, and simple explanations.
- **Save Careers:** Bookmark favorite careers with one click (saved in browser `localStorage`).
- **Dark / Light Mode:** Switch between dark and light themes anytime.
- **Mobile Friendly:** Works smoothly on phones, tablets, and laptops.
- **100% Free:** No paid APIs, no sign-in required, and no database needed.

## 🚀 How to Run the Project

1. Download or clone this folder.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click on **`index.html`**.
4. The website will open in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)!

## 🌐 How to Host Online for Free

### 1. Using Netlify Drop (Easiest - 1 Minute):
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the `CareerPath` folder into the box.
3. You will get a free live link to share with your college!

### 2. Using GitHub Pages:
1. Create a new repository on GitHub named `careerpath`.
2. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and `README.md`.
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > Select branch `main` > Click **Save**.
4. Your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/careerpath/`.
